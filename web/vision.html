Why two devices today, not this?

Today's demo uses two devices because no ordinary app can hear an Android or iOS call (that needs a system-level permission only a phone maker or carrier can grant). This page shows the vision for what Riposte feels like once it can join the call itself — call merge/forwarding (e.g. Twilio), a carrier network feature, or a phone-maker partnership. No real call, audio or AI runs on this page.
